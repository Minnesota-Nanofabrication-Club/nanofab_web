Semiconductor fabrication is a fight against dirt. A single speck of dust is bigger than the features we want to print, and one stray fingerprint can ruin a wafer. So before any of our machines can do real work, the space they live in has to be clean.

Members spent a day clearing out the fab: hauling away old equipment and boxes, scrubbing benches and floors, and sorting what was left into a space we can actually work in.

## Why it matters

Industrial fabs filter their air until it holds fewer than a handful of particles per cubic foot. We are not building a full cleanroom. Instead, the plan is to put clean-air enclosures around each tool (see the [cleanroom microenvironment](../../machines/#cleanroom-microenvironment) project). Those only work if the room around them starts out clean, organized and easy to keep that way.
