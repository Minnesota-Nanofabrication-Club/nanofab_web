## What we are building

The officers opened with the goal: a working integrated circuit made entirely in a fab the club designs and builds itself, by Spring 2027. That means every step a wafer goes through, from cleaning and oxidation to lithography, etching, doping, metal and testing, on machines students are building right now. The [machines page](../../machines/) walks through each step and the tool that does it.

## How the club works

Every machine has a project team and a lead. Projects are grouped into bands: **Gen One Fab**, the line we are building first; **Gen Two Fab**, the next generation with smaller features; and **ChemE R&D**, which makes our own chemicals. **Operations & Logistics** keeps the club running: money, outreach, design and this website.

No experience is needed. Most members had never been inside a cleanroom before joining, and every team has room for people who want to learn.
