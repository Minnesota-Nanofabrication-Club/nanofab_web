Machine photos go in this folder — one or more per machine, showing
what the tool actually looks like.

NAMING
Name each file after the machine, lowercase with hyphens, no spaces.
If you have several shots of one machine, number them:

  tube-furnace-1.jpg
  tube-furnace-2.jpg
  reactive-ion-etcher-1.jpg

HOOKING THEM UP
Open that machine's file in content/machines/ and add a "photos:"
block to the top section:

  photos:
    - src: "/images/machines/tube-furnace-1.jpg"
      caption: "The furnace mid-assembly, before insulation."
    - src: "/images/machines/tube-furnace-2.jpg"
      caption: "Quartz tube and wafer boat."

One photo renders large. Two or more render as a grid.
"caption" is optional — leave it out and the photo shows on its own.

SIZE
Aim for about 1600 pixels on the long edge and under 500 KB per file.
Phone photos are much larger than that; https://squoosh.app will
shrink one for free with no visible quality loss.

Landscape (wider than tall) photos look best.

Delete this text file once you've added real photos.
