Officer photos go in this folder.

Name each file after the person, lowercase with hyphens and no spaces:
  jane-smith.jpg

Then point at it from data/officers.yaml:
  photo: "/images/officers/jane-smith.jpg"

Square photos work best — the site crops them to a circle. Around
400 x 400 pixels is plenty. Keep each file under about 500 KB;
https://squoosh.app will shrink one for free.

If you don't have someone's photo, leave photo: "" and their card
shows their initials instead.

Delete this text file once you've added real photos.
